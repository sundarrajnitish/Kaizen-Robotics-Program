Tachometer

Level 2 · Arduino · IR sensor · 16x2 LCD

An IR sensor faces a rotating shaft that carries one reflective marker. Every time the marker passes, the sensor output goes high. The sketch counts these pulses for a fixed time window and converts the count to RPM.

Scope: sensor output on D18 (high while the marker is in front of the sensor). Tip: use Speed 4× to skip through the 15 s window.

The maths

The sketch computes RPM = pulses * 12 / 3, which is pulses × 4. With one marker per revolution that means the counting window is 60 s / 4 = 15 s, and the result can only change in steps of 4 RPM.

How it works

  1. The LCD asks you to press the button on D10 (it uses the internal pull-up, so a press reads 0).
  2. count_pulses() loops 1000 × 1227 times. Each time it sees D18 go high it adds one and waits for the marker to pass.
  3. After the window it shows "Please Wait", then the RPM for 5 seconds, then asks again.
